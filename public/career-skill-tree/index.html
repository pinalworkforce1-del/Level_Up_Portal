DESTINATION • LMI LAB

Turn “that sounds cool” into an informed question.

Labor Market Information (LMI) helps you test whether an opportunity exists, what it may pay, how people enter it, and what the market is doing.

💵 WAGES

What might the work pay?

Look beyond one number. Entry, median, and experienced wages can tell different stories.

📈 DEMAND

Is the market growing?

Growth rate shows direction. It does not tell you how many jobs exist.

🚪 OPENINGS

How often are opportunities expected?

Openings can come from growth and workers leaving or changing occupations.

🎓 PREPARATION

What does entry usually require?

Compare education, credentials, licenses, apprenticeships, and experience—not just college versus no college.

⚠️ LMI trap: Industry growth is not the same thing as occupation growth.

A growing healthcare industry employs nurses—but also IT staff, accountants, maintenance workers, drivers, and many other occupations. Always verify the occupation itself.
