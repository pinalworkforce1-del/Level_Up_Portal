# Career Tree — O*NET Mini Interest Profiler Visual Brief

## Purpose
Create 30 consistent Level Up-style work-activity illustrations for the embedded O*NET Mini Interest Profiler. The image supports comprehension and engagement; it must not change, reinterpret, or replace the official O*NET item text.

## File contract
- `onet-01.webp` through `onet-30.webp`
- Landscape 16:9 preferred; minimum 1280×720 before WebP conversion
- No words, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, scores, or answer cues inside the image
- Keep the primary action obvious at thumbnail size
- Leave moderate negative space so the activity is readable without visual clutter

## Shared Level Up art direction
Cinematic graphic-novel realism with polished digital illustration, youthful but not childish, high visual clarity, subtle neon/cyan/violet Level Up lighting accents, rich contrast, believable workplaces, inclusive young-adult subjects, energetic composition, professional safety practices where relevant. Avoid fantasy costumes, exaggerated superhero poses, brand marks, and text. The image should show the work activity neutrally — never make one activity look more prestigious, exciting, unpleasant, or difficult than another.

## Bias / assessment guardrail
All 30 visuals should receive roughly equal production quality, lighting polish, subject confidence, and visual energy. Do not make one RIASEC area consistently brighter, cooler, more glamorous, or more stressful than another. The participant should be rating the work activity — not reacting to a visual quality cue.
