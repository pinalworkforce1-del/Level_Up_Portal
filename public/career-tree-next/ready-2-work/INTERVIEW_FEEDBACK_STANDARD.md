# Interview Intelligence — Nova Feedback Standard (approved)

## Approved report layout — do not change without review

1. Conversation Balance — candidate versus Jacqueline speaking time, 80/20 as a developmental guideline only.
2. Nova's Interview Overview — direct second-person summary.
3. Your Questions and Growth Opportunities — one card per required question:
   - **Question**
   - **Your response** (faithful to transcript)
   - **Growth opportunity** (AI generated, specific, constructive, 2–3 concise sentences)
4. Nova's Suggested Practice Goals — two or three personalized priorities.
5. Print / Save Reflection as PDF.

No duplicate rule-based question-by-question reflections and no repetitive generic checklist.

## AI coaching requirements

- Talk to the participant directly as **you/your**, not “the candidate.”
- Each **Growth opportunity** must include **one concrete way to improve that exact response**, naming the kind of real detail or example that would help (e.g., a specific role responsibility, a personal action, or a method for handling competing priorities).
- Tie that suggestion to the actual question and the person's own words. Keep it brief and avoid repeating the same advice across questions.
- Never fabricate work experience, achievements, qualifications, or outcomes. Ask for a missing detail rather than supplying it as fact.
- Identify strengths when deserved; do not force criticism into strong answers.
- Treat 80/20 as coaching guidance, not a completion requirement or pass/fail metric.
- Use verified transcript and per-speaker timing where available; distinguish approximate word-count balance from measured speaking time.
- Only show participant feedback after evidence confirms all required interview questions were answered and the session closed naturally; explicit early exit does not unlock feedback.
- Preserve billing/session finalization and pilot controls separately.
